How we play

Two ways to
hit the sand.

2s

Competitive doubles beach volleyball with a friendly atmosphere. Teams of 2M, 1M1F, or 2F are welcome.

Played by the book, but nobody is taking themselves too seriously.

4s

Grab three friends and get on the sand. Modified four-person rules, beach games, and a strong emphasis on having a good time.

Best vibes win.

About

This started
with a game.

Emma's Beach Volleyball started with a group of friends getting together to play. It grew into tournaments, new teams, new friends, and full days on the beach.

The goal is simple: make beach volleyball accessible, competitive, social, and worth spending a Saturday on.
